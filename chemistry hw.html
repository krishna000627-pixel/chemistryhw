1 Write and Learn: Named Reactions

15 Reactions

1. Wurtz Reaction

Alkanes Synthesis

Treatment of alkyl halides with sodium metal in dry ether gives higher alkanes containing twice the number of carbon atoms.

2CH₃Cl + 2Na ──(dry ether)──> CH₃–CH₃ + 2NaCl

2. Frankland Reaction

Zinc Coupling

Alkyl halides are heated with zinc metal instead of sodium to form higher alkanes.

2C₂H₅I + Zn ──(Δ)──> C₄H₁₀ + ZnI₂

3. Swarts Reaction

Fluorination

Alkyl fluorides are prepared by heating alkyl chlorides or bromides with metallic fluorides.

CH₃Cl + AgF ──> CH₃F + AgCl↓

4. Ullmann Reaction

Aryl Coupling

Aryl halides react with copper powder at high temperature to form biphenyl compounds.

2C₆H₅I + Cu ──(Δ)──> C₆H₅–C₆H₅ + Cu₂I₂

5. Hunsdiecker Reaction

Decarboxylation

Silver salts of carboxylic acids react with halogens in CCl₄ to form alkyl halides with decarboxylation.

CH₃COOAg + Br₂ ──(CCl₄, Δ)──> CH₃Br + CO₂ + AgBr

6. Reimer-Tiemann Reaction

Phenol Formylation

Phenol reacts with chloroform and aqueous alkali to introduce an aldehyde group at the ortho position.

C₆H₅OH + CHCl₃ + 3NaOH ──> C₆H₄(OH)(CHO) + 3NaCl + 2H₂O

7. Rosenmund Reaction

Aldehyde Synthesis

Acyl chlorides are hydrogenated over Pd/BaSO₄ catalyst to form aldehydes.

C₆H₅COCl + H₂ ──(Pd/BaSO₄)──> C₆H₅CHO + HCl

8. Williamson Synthesis

Ether Synthesis

An alkyl halide reacts with a sodium alkoxide to form symmetrical or unsymmetrical ethers.

CH₃CH₂Cl + C₂H₅ONa ──> CH₃CH₂–O–CH₂CH₃ + NaCl

9. Williamson Continuous

Ether Process

An excess of primary alcohol reacts with concentrated sulfuric acid at a controlled temperature to continuously produce symmetrical ethers.

2CH₃CH₂OH ──(conc. H₂SO₄, 413 K)──> CH₃CH₂–O–CH₂CH₃ + H₂O

10. Gattermann Koch Reaction

Benzaldehyde Route

Benzene reacts with carbon monoxide and hydrogen chloride in the presence of anhydrous AlCl₃ to form benzaldehyde.

C₆H₆ + CO + HCl ──(anhydrous AlCl₃)──> C₆H₅CHO + HCl

11. Gattermann Aldehyde

Formylation

Benzene reacts with hydrogen cyanide (HCN) and HCl in the presence of a Lewis acid to yield benzaldehyde.

C₆H₆ + HCN + HCl ──(AlCl₃)──> C₆H₅CH=NH ──(H₂O)──> C₆H₅CHO + NH₄Cl

12. Etard Reaction

Chromyl Chloride

Toluene is oxidized using chromyl chloride (CrO₂Cl₂) to form a chromium complex, which hydrolyzes to benzaldehyde.

C₆H₅CH₃ + 2CrO₂Cl₂ ──(CS₂)──> Chromium complex ──(H₃O⁺)──> C₆H₅CHO

13. Aldol Condensation

Self-Condensation

Aldehydes with α-hydrogen atoms undergo self-condensation in dilute alkali to form β-hydroxy aldehydes.

2CH₃CHO ──(dil. NaOH)──> CH₃–CH(OH)–CH₂–CHO

14. Cannizzaro Reaction

Disproportionation

Aldehydes lacking α-hydrogens undergo disproportionation with concentrated alkali.

2HCHO + conc. NaOH ──> CH₃OH + HCOONa

15. Benzoin Condensation

Aromatic Ketones

Aromatic aldehydes react in the presence of alcoholic potassium cyanide to form α-hydroxy ketones.

2C₆H₅CHO ──(alcoholic KCN)──> C₆H₅–CH(OH)–CO–C₆H₅

16. Chloromethane to Chloroethane & Vice Versa

Chloromethane → Chloroethane

1. 2CH₃Cl + 2Na →(dry ether)→ C₂H₆ + 2NaCl
2. C₂H₆ + Cl₂ →(hν)→ C₂H₅Cl + HCl

Chloroethane → Chloromethane

1. CH₃CH₂Cl + KOH(alc.) → CH₂=CH₂ + KCl + H₂O
2. CH₂=CH₂ + H₂O →(H⁺)→ CH₃CH₂OH
3. CH₃CH₂OH →([O])→ CH₃COOH
4. CH₃COOH + NaOH → CH₃COONa + H₂O
5. CH₃COONa + NaOH →(Δ)→ CH₄ + Na₂CO₃
6. CH₄ + Cl₂ →(hν)→ CH₃Cl + HCl

17. Methanol to Ethanol & Vice Versa

Methanol → Ethanol

1. CH₃OH + PCl₅ → CH₃Cl + POCl₃ + HCl
2. CH₃Cl + KCN(alc.) → CH₃CN + KCl
3. CH₃CN + 4[H] → CH₃CH₂NH₂
4. CH₃CH₂NH₂ + HNO₂ → CH₃CH₂OH + N₂ + H₂O

Ethanol → Methanol

1. CH₃CH₂OH →(KMnO₄/H⁺)→ CH₃COOH
2. CH₃COONa + NaOH →(Δ)→ CH₄ + Na₂CO₃
3. CH₄ + Cl₂ →(hν)→ CH₃Cl + HCl
4. CH₃Cl + KOH(aq) → CH₃OH + KCl

18. Ethanol to Ethoxyethane & Vice Versa

Ethanol → Ethoxyethane

2CH₃CH₂OH ──(conc. H₂SO₄, 413 K)──> CH₃CH₂–O–CH₂CH₃ + H₂O

Ethoxyethane → Ethanol

CH₃CH₂–O–CH₂CH₃ + HI ──(Δ)──> 2CH₃CH₂OH
